Privacy Policy

How QantaraTools handles local processing, browser storage, external resources, and privacy choices.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. What QantaraTools does

QantaraTools is a free collection of browser-based utilities. The current release has no intentionally integrated advertising SDKs or pop-up advertising. Some tools load interface or processing libraries from third-party CDNs, and the Currency Converter requests public exchange-rate data when live rates are needed.

2. Local processing and files

Many text, image, PDF, document, and developer tools process selected files or text in your browser. A file selected for a local tool is not intentionally uploaded to QantaraTools servers by that tool. This is not a blanket promise for every future plugin or every external-service feature, so read the tool description and network behavior before using sensitive material.

3. Analytics, external requests, and providers

The site asks for consent before loading Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and understand general usage, using measurement ID G-0CKBXQ6WXX. If you choose “Only necessary,” Analytics is not loaded. You can clear this site’s browser storage to see the choice again. Google may process information such as page views, approximate location, device/browser details, and interaction events according to its policies. The site also uses third-party resources such as Google Fonts, Font Awesome, Tailwind browser assets, PDF/QR/EXIF helper libraries, and a public exchange-rate endpoint. Your browser may contact those providers to retrieve a resource or live data. Their privacy practices and availability are governed by their own policies.

4. Tool ratings and feedback

When you submit a tool rating, QantaraTools uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Firestore to store your rating centrally so an aggregate score can be shown to other visitors. An anonymous Firebase identifier is used; you do not need to create an account. Optional written notes are stored separately and are only readable by that anonymous browser identity. Clearing browser data or using another browser may create a different anonymous identity.

4. Browser storage

The site may store theme, visual identity, favorites, recently used tools, and tool requests in localStorage on your device. These values are convenience data, not an account profile. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove them. The site does not require an account for the current toolkit.

5. Information we do not request

Do not enter passwords, private keys, payment-card data, confidential business information, regulated data, or other sensitive material unless you have verified that the specific tool’s processing method is appropriate. QantaraTools does not intentionally ask for such information.

6. Security and retention

Local data remains under your browser’s control until you clear it. No service can guarantee absolute security, and third-party resources can change. Keep your browser updated and verify important outputs before relying on them.

7. Children and contact

The toolkit is general-purpose and is not directed at children. For support or privacy questions, contact support@qantaratools.com. Do not email passwords, private keys, or confidential files.

8. Changes

This policy may change when tools, plugins, hosting, analytics, advertising, or external providers change. The date above identifies the current version.

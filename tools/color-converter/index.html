Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between RGB and CMYK?

RGB mixes light and is used for screens, while CMYK mixes ink and is used for print. Converted values are close approximations because the two color spaces cover different ranges.

How do I convert HEX to RGB?

Enter the HEX code, for example #1E90FF, and the tool shows the matching RGB, HSL and CMYK values you can copy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Divide the gross price by 1 plus the VAT rate (for example 1.20 for 20 percent) to get the net price.

Does it use my country rate?

You enter the rate yourself, so it works for any VAT or sales tax percentage.

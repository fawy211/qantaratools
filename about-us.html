About QantaraTools

Useful tools.
Less busywork.

QantaraTools is a free collection of practical utilities for developers, creators, and anyone who wants to get everyday digital work done a little faster.

Our mission

Make common tasks simple: clean up text and data, convert formats, inspect files, and solve small workflow problems without installing another app or creating an account.

Built for quick wins

Open a tool, do the task, and get back to your work. The toolkit is free to use and designed to stay clear, approachable, and useful across devices.

Privacy, plainly stated

Many tools process text and files locally in your browser, so those inputs are not intentionally uploaded by those tools. Some features do contact external services: for example, community ratings are stored in Firebase, and analytics loads only if you consent. Check the Privacy Policy for the full details, especially before using sensitive data with a tool that calls an external service.

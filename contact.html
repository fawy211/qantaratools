Contact Us

Need help with a tool, found a problem, or have a suggestion? We would be happy to hear from you.

QantaraTools Support

For support, bug reports, feedback, and general questions, email us at:

support@qantaratools.com

Please include the tool name and a short description of the issue. If relevant, include your browser, operating system, and approximate file size. Do not send passwords, private keys, or confidential information.

Tool Suggestions

Have an idea for a useful new tool? Use the “Request a New Tool” option on the QantaraTools homepage or email your suggestion to the support address.

Before You Contact Us

  • Refresh the page and try the tool again.
  • Use a current browser.
  • For file tools, try a smaller sample file.
  • Tell us exactly which step failed and what you expected to happen.
